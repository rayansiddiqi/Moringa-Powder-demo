The miracle tree

A gift from India's living soil

Known in Ayurveda as shigru, moringa has nourished Indian households for generations. Every part of this resilient tree has a purpose—but its delicate leaves are where extraordinary nutrition gathers.

Our leaves are harvested at their greenest, shade-dried at low temperatures, and milled in small batches. The result is a vivid powder with a clean, earthy taste and nothing to hide.
